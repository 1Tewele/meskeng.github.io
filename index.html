About MESK

MESK is an Ethiopian consulting firm built by senior practitioners in highway engineering, hydrology and hydraulics, bridge and structural engineering, and social and environmental safeguards.

Our partners have spent their careers on federal, regional and city road programmes, working with design consultants, supervision consultants and Design and Build contractors. We understand how designs are built, checked and paid for.

Each core discipline is led by a partner, so the full road-project team sits under one roof. We work to whichever national or international standards a project requires.

How we work

Every assignment follows the same seven steps, so clients receive consistent and verifiable results.

  1. 01

    Inception

    Terms of reference, data and client requirements reviewed; standards and schedule agreed.

  2. 02

    Site investigation

    Joint site visit to assess technical, environmental and social conditions, including crossings, structures, hydrology and access.

  3. 03

    Option studies

    Alternatives compared on cost, feasibility, constructability and maintenance.

  4. 04

    Analysis and design

    Verified models, calculations to the agreed standards, and drawings.

  5. 05

    Independent check

    Every calculation and drawing checked by an engineer who did not prepare it.

  6. 06

    Quantities and documents

    Bill of quantities, cost estimate, specifications and design report.

  7. 07

    Review and close-out

    Comments answered; support through tender and construction.
